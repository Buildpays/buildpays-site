# Surface brief: homepage (public/index.html)

Scope: the homepage, on branch `redesign-board` — the first surface of a replacement visual world for the whole site
(then /construction, /ap-automation, /square, /integrations, /partners). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: three, equally — the owner or office manager of a Melbourne construction subcontractor on a CFMEU agreement; the
owner of any Australian small business (a shop on Square, a trade, a cafe); the accountant or bookkeeper who looks after
them. Job: see in one screen that PayKicker reads what the business already produces (hours, supplier bills, Square sales,
bank lines) and writes it into Xero or MYOB coded — and which door is theirs. Action: Book a demo (form `#contact`, the one
orange action) or Start free trial (Invoices, secondary). Proof: the board flipping entries to CODED, then the real product
on screen, large. TR's words this round: "too plain / looks like a document, not a product; too corporate / cold" — wants
presence, warmth, confidence, the product up front. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented figures, no customers or
testimonials, independence line, legal footer, CSP, analytics wiring, logo locked); copy verified 12 Oct 2026 in the plan
file; "other apps", never Amaka; Yo More never named; example businesses Southline Formwork Pty Ltd and an unnamed Brunswick
shop.

Unresolved: whether exhibits are drawn (mock) or captured from the real app (build).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A departures board for your books. Every entry a business produces is a row on the board, and overnight each row
flips, flap by flap, to CODED with its gate: the Xero or MYOB account, job and GST. Nothing waits on the owner; the board
keeps flipping. It refuses the SaaS hero with a framed screenshot, the three-card grid, and the glowing neon-on-black look.

OWN-WORLD: The board's ground is a deep night navy (#0b1826, the logo navy taken down) across the whole page; flap cells a
step lighter (#15293d) with a 1px hinge line across their middle and 2px corners; flap lettering warm white (#f4ecdc); amber
(#f0b541) for a row in motion and for the time column; CODED in a calm green (#62d08f); the logo orange #f25c1f only on the
primary action. One light: a single warm, soft light bleed behind each product screen (a real radial falloff, no gradient
text, no neon edges). Type: a condensed grotesk in caps for the board (a face with character from Google Fonts — not
Oswald/Inter/Space Grotesk; the builder verifies tabular figures) and a humanist sans for reading. Composition: the board
is a full-width grid of columns TIME · ENTRY · AMOUNT · GATE (account · job · GST) · STATUS; sections below are "gates":
one large real product screen each, no device chrome, on the dark ground; the three doors are a strip of three board rows,
not cards. Controls: flat rectangles, no radius beyond 2px; links underlined.

STORY: The visitor lands on a board already mid-flip: WAGES, a supplier bill, a day of Square sales, a transfer — each
settles to CODED with its gate. They understand the promise before reading a sentence, believe it when the gates below show
the actual app doing it, find their door on the strip and book a demo or start the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav on the night ground: logo (reversed variant), EBA Construction · AP automation · Square sales ·
Integrations · Partners, Log in (hairline), Book a demo (orange). Headline in warm white, large and short: "Your books.
Coded overnight." Strap: "Hours, supplier bills and Square sales read, coded and posted into Xero or MYOB while you sleep."
Then the board, full width, four rows flipping in on load (reduced motion: at rest): 03 JUL · WAGES — EBA PAY RUN W27 ·
38,412.60 · 6-1000 WAGES · WGT01 · CODED; 04 JUL · WESTGATE REO SUPPLIES INV 44821 · 4,312.00 · 6-2100 MATERIALS · GST ·
WGT01 · CODED; 07 JUL · SQUARE — 212 ORDERS, BRUNSWICK · 1,842.00 · 4-1000 SALES · GST · BRUNSWICK · CODED; 07 JUL ·
TRANSFER TO SAVINGS · 20,000.00 · PAIRED 0119 · COMING (bank coding is not live). Under the board, the doors strip.

FORM: The split-flap departures board, position 5 on the round-2 grounded list (signwriter, the app is the site, dawn on
site, polymer banknote, split-flap board, PTV wayfinding, pay packet); seed key 92ce78f0, kind assigned. Raises from the
declined challengers: a continuous circuit (the flip sequence is one orchestrated run, never per-element loops); the
archive wall's monospaced runs (amounts and gates in strict tabular columns); the oscilloscope's graticule (a faint
measured grid behind the board, one line weight); the ink basin's real physics (flap motion with real easing and a settle,
never a snap); the collective's fixed palette (five colours, no more); the poster's ranger-voiced imperative (one short
imperative headline). Signature interaction: the flip — rows resolve once, in sequence, with a soft flap tick as the
motion's only sound-free rhythm; after settling the board stays.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md rewritten
from the built world, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance and a synthetic-data label.
