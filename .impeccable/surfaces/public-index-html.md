# Surface brief: homepage (public/index.html)

Scope: the homepage, on branch `redesign-signwriter` — the first surface of a replacement visual world for the whole site
(then /construction, /ap-automation, /square, /integrations, /partners). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: three, equally — the owner or office manager of a Melbourne construction subcontractor on a CFMEU agreement; the
owner of any Australian small business (a shop on Square, a trade, a cafe); the accountant or bookkeeper who looks after
them. Job: see in one screen that PayKicker reads what the business already produces (hours, supplier bills, Square sales,
bank lines) and writes it into Xero or MYOB coded — and which door is theirs. Action: Book a demo (form `#contact`, the one
orange action) or Start free trial (Invoices, secondary). Proof: the product in the window, large and real. TR's words this
round: "too plain / looks like a document, not a product; too corporate / cold" — wants presence, warmth, confidence, the
product up front. Constraints: PRODUCT.md (no invented figures, no customers or testimonials, independence line, legal
footer, CSP, analytics wiring, logo locked); copy verified 12 Oct 2026 in the plan file; "other apps", never Amaka; Yo More
never named; example businesses Southline Formwork Pty Ltd and an unnamed Brunswick shop.

Unresolved: whether exhibits are drawn (mock) or captured from the real app (build).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shopfront. Melbourne's signwriters put a business's name on the fascia and the ute door with confidence and
warmth, and the window shows the goods. PayKicker's homepage is that shopfront: the name set large across a navy fascia,
and in the window the product itself, lit and at full size. It refuses the SaaS hero with a framed screenshot, the
three-card grid, and retro pastiche (no faux brush textures, no distressed type, no badges).

OWN-WORLD: Fascia and footer in logo navy #12293f; the window ground a warm white (#fbf7f0 — the world's own warmth, kept
far from cream-and-serif pastiche by modern type and flat colour); lettering in navy; orange #f25c1f for the primary action
and the signwriter's one highlight line; a hairline gold rule (#c9a24a) as the window's only ornament. Flat: no shadows,
no gradients, no radius. Type: a bold gothic with real character for the fascia and headlines (Google Fonts; not
Oswald/Inter/Space Grotesk/Bebas) paired with a Clarendon-style slab for secondary lines — the signwriter's pairing — and a
humanist sans for reading; figures tabular. Composition: the window is a wide pane divided by two mullions into three
lights, one per door, each light holding a real product screen at large size (no device chrome) with its signwritten
title on the glass; sections below are further windows down the street: AP automation, Square sales, Construction crews,
Accountants, each one window with one screen. Controls: flat navy or orange rectangles, hairline-gold rules; links
underlined.

STORY: The visitor sees a confident, warm shopfront with the product in the window — the Invoices inbox, the Sales day
summaries, the morning roster — and reads one line of signwriting that says what it does. They pick their light (their
door), see the screen for it, and book a demo or start the trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The fascia: logo (reversed) left, EBA Construction · AP automation · Square sales · Integrations · Partners,
Log in (hairline), Book a demo (orange). Below the fascia on the warm white: the headline signwritten large and tight:
"Your books, done." then the slab line "Hours, supplier bills and Square sales — read, coded and posted into Xero or MYOB."
Then the window: three lights, left to right — "Construction crews" (the roster board screen), "Any business" (the Invoices
bill screen, with a supplier bill read and coded), "Accountants" (the partner page screen: a client list and this month's
15%), each with its one-line door copy and link. Under the window, the gold rule and the line "Prices include GST · 14 days
free on invoices · No lock-in."

FORM: The signwriter, position 1 on the round-2 grounded list (signwriter, the app is the site, dawn on site, polymer
banknote, split-flap board, PTV wayfinding, pay packet); seed key 92ce78f0, kind pick (the roll dealt 5, the split-flap
board, built beside this one). Raises from the declined challengers: a continuous circuit (one orchestrated reveal, the
window lighting once); the archive wall's monospaced runs (figures in strict tabular columns); the oscilloscope's graticule
(the window's mullions and gold rule on one measured grid); the ink basin's real physics (the one motion has real easing);
the collective's fixed palette (five colours, no more); the poster's imperative (one short confident headline). Signature
interaction: the window lights — on load the three lights brighten in sequence from warm-dim to full, once; reduced motion
shows them lit.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md rewritten
from the built world, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance and a synthetic-data label.
