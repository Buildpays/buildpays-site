APartners

Refer a business. Earn 15% for a year.

Accountants, bookkeepers, consultants — anyone can be a PayKicker referral partner. When a business signs up with your link, you earn 15% of what it pays PayKicker for its first 12 months, and the business gets 75% off its first 3 bills.

Become a partner

BHow it works

Three steps

  1. Sign up at app.paykicker.com.au/partners with your name, email and the bank account your fees go to. Confirm your email from the link we send.
  2. Share your link. Your page shows your code and a sign-up link with it built in. A business can also type your code when it signs up.
  3. Get paid monthly. Each month we email a statement and pay what you've earned by direct deposit.

What you earn

  • 15% of the bill total (including GST) the business pays, on each of its first 12 bills that carry a charge.
  • On every PayKicker subscription the business takes: EBA payroll, supplier invoices, the Square sales sync.
  • The business stays yours: once it signs up with your code it is tagged to you for good.
  • Earned when the business pays its bill. A month with nothing to pay (a free tier or a trial) doesn't use up one of the 12.

How you're paid

  • By direct deposit to your bank account, each month from the 8th, once $50 is owed. Less than that carries forward to the next month.
  • When a business's 12th bill is paid, we pay whatever is owed, even under $50.
  • The amount is GST-inclusive: nothing is added on top and you don't send us an invoice. We email a statement with every payment.
  • Your page shows every business you referred, what each has earned you, and every payment.

What the business gets

75% off its first 3 bills that carry a charge. Its price is not higher because it was referred. We tell the business that you receive a referral fee — at sign-up, in its welcome email and on its billing page — and you agree to tell them too.

Questions

Do I need an ABN? No. Anyone can be a partner.

Do I charge GST? No invoice is needed and the fee is GST-inclusive. Your own tax on what you receive is yours to handle; ask your accountant.

Can I refer my own business? No — a sign-up with the partner's own email isn't tagged.
