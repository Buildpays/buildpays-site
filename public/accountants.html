Questions.

Who owns the client's account?

Your client does. It gets an invite, stays the owner and can remove your firm at any time.

Do I approve invoices?

Only if the owner ticks Approve or names your firm on an approval path. The owner decides.

Can I connect their Xero or MYOB?

Yes, if you're an Adviser or Standard user on the client's Xero, or have access to its MYOB company file. Square needs the owner to add you on Square with full permissions; if they haven't, the owner gets a “Connect your Square” link.

Who pays?

You choose for each client. If your firm pays, you get one monthly tax invoice at the full price, listing each client your firm pays for. If the client pays, it adds its own card.

How is my fee paid?

Monthly, by direct deposit, once $50 is owed; less than that carries forward to the next month. The fee is GST-inclusive: nothing is added on top and you don't send us an invoice. If your firm pays for clients, your fees are set against your firm's monthly bill as a credit instead, and credit left over is paid by direct deposit once it reaches $50.

I already refer clients. What changes?

Nothing, unless you want it to. Turn your partner link into a practice account and keep your code, the businesses you referred and your earnings. A business you referred doesn't share its books with your firm unless its owner adds your firm.
