What stays the same in MYOB

  • Accounts. Set it up for me uses the Square accounts already in your file. It never makes a second one.
  • Customers. Each shop’s day goes to the shop’s customer already there, such as one Amaka made.
  • Jobs. Every line, and the shop’s Square fees, go on the shop’s job.
  • Entries. One tax-inclusive invoice per shop per day. Each payout is a transfer to your bank, with Square’s fees as one spend money.

Xero works the same way, with a tracking option per shop.

Switch in a week

  1. Connect MYOB or Xero: the same file Amaka posts to.
  2. Connect Square. Leave Amaka running.
  3. Answer Yes under Before posting. PayKicker finds the last day Amaka posted.
  4. Press Set it up for me, then choose Not yet. For a week PayKicker reads every day and posts nothing.
  5. Compare, then switch. Turn Amaka’s Square sync off and pick PayKicker’s first day. A day Amaka already posted is skipped.

Questions

Will my old days double up?

No. PayKicker finds the last day Amaka posted for each shop, and skips any day or payout already in your file, even while Amaka is still running.

Do I lose Amaka's history?

No. Everything Amaka posted stays in MYOB or Xero as it is. PayKicker posts from the day you choose.

What about my other Amaka integrations?

PayKicker covers Square, plus Uber Eats and DoorDash payouts. Shopify is coming. You can keep Amaka for anything else and move only your Square sales.
