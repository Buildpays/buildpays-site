What stays the same for your bookkeeper

If Amaka sends your Square days to MYOB as a daily summary, the file your bookkeeper reconciles keeps its shape.

  • The same accounts. Set it up for me looks in your MYOB file for the Square accounts already there, by their usual names (Square Sales, Square Discounts, Square Card Surcharges, Square Tips, Square Gift Cards, Square Balance, Square Cash Clearing, Square Undeposited Funds, Square Fees), and uses them. It never makes an account whose name already exists.
  • The same customers. Each shop’s day is an invoice to the shop’s customer. A customer already in your file with the shop’s name, such as one Amaka made, is reused, never doubled, and you can pick any other customer from the list.
  • The same jobs. Every line goes on the shop’s job, found by the shop’s name or picked from the list, and Square’s fees for that shop go on the same job.
  • The same kind of entry. One tax-inclusive sales invoice per shop per day. When your file has a Square Undeposited Funds account, the day’s takings go in as one payment there and a journal moves them on to Square Balance, cash clearing and the gift card account.
  • The same lines to reconcile. Each Square payout reaches your bank as a transfer from Square Balance, the line your bank feed matches, and what Square kept comes out of Square Balance onto your fees account with its GST, as one spend money. If your bookkeeper is used to a different kind of entry for the fees, the accounts stay the same; only the entry type may change.

In Xero, PayKicker posts one tax-inclusive invoice per shop per day on the shop’s jobs tracking option, a payment into each account the money arrived in, and each payout settled the same way. Set it beside what Amaka posts in your file during the read-only week.

How to switch in a week

A week beside Amaka with PayKicker posting nothing, then one day where PayKicker takes over.

  1. Start free and connect your file. Sign up with MYOB or Xero and connect the same file Amaka posts to.
  2. Connect Square. Sign in to Square and press Allow. PayKicker only reads from Square. Leave Amaka running.
  3. Answer Yes under Before posting. PayKicker asks whether another app or a bookkeeper has been putting your Square sales into the file. Say yes, and it looks in the file for the last day Amaka posted for each shop.
  4. Press Set it up for me on each shop. It finds the Square accounts, the shop’s customer and its job already in your file. Then pick the bank account Square pays into.
  5. Choose Not yet for a week. Under Start posting, pick Not yet: PayKicker reads every day and posts nothing, while Amaka keeps posting as before.
  6. Compare the days. Each day in PayKicker shows its sales, GST, card and cash, checked against Square’s own totals. Set them beside the invoices Amaka posted, shop by shop.
  7. Turn Amaka’s Square sync off, then choose PayKicker’s first day. Once Amaka has posted its last day, switch off its Square sync to that file. In PayKicker choose From a date I choose, the first day Amaka did not post, and switch on Post each day by itself. If Amaka put a day in after all, PayKicker sees it in the file and skips that day.

Questions

Will my old days double up?

No. When you tell PayKicker under Before posting that another app has been posting your Square sales, it finds the last day Amaka posted for each shop, and from then on a day or payout Amaka already put in the file is skipped, never posted again, even while Amaka is still running. Nothing posts before the start date you choose, and PayKicker finds its own entries again before writing, so a retry never doubles.

Do I lose Amaka's history?

No. Everything Amaka posted stays in MYOB or Xero as it is. PayKicker posts from the day you choose. If you want a month before the switch lined up with Square, PayKicker can compare it with Square shop by shop and day by day and, only when you confirm, post one adjusting entry for it, for any month of this financial year.

Can I keep MYOB or Xero as it is?

Yes. MYOB or Xero stays your books: the same file, accounts, customers and jobs. Set it up for me uses the Square accounts already in your file and never makes an account whose name already exists. PayKicker only reads from Square and never moves money.

Can I run PayKicker beside Amaka first?

Yes. Choose Not yet when PayKicker asks when to start posting: it reads every day from Square and posts nothing, so you can compare each day with what Amaka posted before you pick PayKicker's first day.

What about my other Amaka integrations?

PayKicker's sales sync covers Square, plus Uber Eats and DoorDash payouts from their weekly statements. Shopify is coming but not open yet, and other sales systems are not covered. If Amaka also syncs something else for you, you can keep Amaka for that and move only your Square sales; check with Amaka what that means for your plan.
