PayKicker vs Lightyear for supplier invoices

Both read supplier invoices with their line items, run approvals and post bills into Xero or MYOB. Lightyear is built for larger finance teams, with purchase orders raised inside it, goods received notes and inventory on its higher plan. PayKicker does the supplier-bill job, approvals, purchase-order matching and supplier statements included, for a price that follows how many bills you had that month. Here is the side by side, with Lightyear's figures from its own Australian pricing page.

What the price difference is

Lightyear's smallest plan is $155 a month, whether you have 20 bills or 125. PayKicker charges for the band each month lands in: $9.99 up to 20, $29.99 up to 100, $69.99 up to 400, $149 up to 2,000, GST included. A business with 100 supplier bills a month pays $29.99 against $155: about $1,500 a year. A business with 300 bills pays $69.99 against Lightyear's Standard plan at $255: about $2,200 a year.

Lightyear's page does not say whether its prices include GST, and says a monthly integration fee may apply depending on your accounting software. Check both before you decide.

Which one fits

  • Pick Lightyear if you raise purchase orders inside your AP system rather than in Xero or MYOB, need goods received notes and inventory, or process staff expense claims in the same tool.
  • Pick PayKicker if your job is supplier bills: read with their lines, coded to account, job and tax code, approved by the right person within their limit, matched to the purchase order in your file, and posted to Xero or MYOB, at a price that follows your volume.

Questions

Is PayKicker cheaper than Lightyear?

For most small and mid-sized businesses, yes, by a lot. Lightyear's Essentials plan is $155 a month for 125 credits, read on 10 November 2026; PayKicker is $9.99 a month up to 20 invoices, $29.99 up to 100, $69.99 up to 400 and $149 up to 2,000, GST included. A business with 100 bills a month saves about $1,500 a year. Lightyear's page does not say whether its price includes GST, and says an integration fee may apply.

Does PayKicker read line items like Lightyear does?

Yes. Every invoice is read with its lines, in every band, by two independent readers that must agree on the money. Lines are coded to account, tax code and job, and an invoice that mixes GST and GST-free items is split onto the right tax codes by itself.

Does PayKicker do approvals, purchase orders and supplier statements?

Approvals: paths by job with levels in order, any one or all at each level, an authority limit per approver and delegation for set dates. Purchase orders come from your Xero or MYOB file and are matched to the bill, with a delivery docket photo or MYOB's received quantity as the receipt. Supplier statements emailed in are reconciled against PayKicker and your Xero or MYOB ledger. All included in the price.

Can I switch from Lightyear to PayKicker?

Yes. Connect Xero or MYOB to PayKicker, send new bills to your PayKicker invoices address, and what Lightyear already exported stays in your books. Set up your approval paths and approvers in an afternoon; PayKicker learns your coding from the bills you approve.
