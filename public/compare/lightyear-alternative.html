PayKicker vs Lightyear for supplier invoices

Both read supplier invoices with their lines, get them approved and post them to Xero or MYOB. Lightyear is a mature, well-reviewed accounts payable suite from The Access Group with purchase orders, expenses and a mobile app, sold in monthly credit bundles from $155. PayKicker is built around the supplier bill, the statement and the job, for a price that starts at $9.99 and follows how many bills you had. Here is how the two compare for an Australian business on Xero or MYOB.

What the price buys

Lightyear sells credits. The Essentials bundle is 125 a month for $155, and a bill, a credit note, a statement and a purchase order each spend one. A business with 60 bills, 15 statements and 30 orders a month spends 105 credits and is close to the ceiling; the next bundle is $255. The accounting integration may carry its own monthly fee, and the Australian pricing page does not say whether GST is included.

PayKicker counts supplier invoices and credit notes you read and submit, and nothing else. Statements and purchase orders are part of the price. For up to 20 invoices a month that is $9.99, GST included; up to 100 is $29.99; up to 400 is $69.99; up to 1,000 is $149.

The supplier statement

Both products answer the month-end question, which invoices never arrived. Lightyear flags the missing bills on a statement and offers one click to ask the supplier for a copy.

PayKicker goes a step further: the statement arrives by email at your invoices address and reconciles itself against the bills in PayKicker and the payments and credits already in Xero or MYOB, writes off a small difference within a tolerance you set, and drafts the email asking for the missing invoices with every line's state in plain words. It is part of the price, not a credit.

Which one fits

  • Pick Lightyear if you want staff expense claims and receipt capture in the same tool, a native mobile app, purchase orders raised and sent from inside the AP system, QuickBooks, NetSuite or Sage, or support around the clock on weekdays.
  • Pick PayKicker if your job is supplier bills on Xero or MYOB: read with their lines, GST split, coded to account, job and tax code, approved within limits, the statement reconciled, the order matched to the docket, for a price that follows how many bills you had.

Questions

Is PayKicker cheaper than Lightyear?

For a business with up to 100 supplier invoices a month, by a wide margin. PayKicker is $9.99 a month inc GST up to 20 invoices and $29.99 up to 100. Lightyear's Essentials plan is $155 a month for 125 credits, where a bill, a statement and a purchase order each use a credit, and its Australian pricing page does not say whether GST is included or what an integration costs on top. Read 31 October 2026.

Does Lightyear reconcile supplier statements?

Yes: it checks the statement for missing bills and offers one click to request a copy from the supplier, and each statement uses a credit. PayKicker reconciles the statement that arrives by email against the bills in PayKicker and the payments and credits in Xero or MYOB, within a tolerance you set, drafts the email asking for the missing invoices, and counts no extra charge.

Which has the better mobile experience?

Lightyear has a mobile app for capturing receipts and approving bills. PayKicker is a web app built for a phone first: approvers open, approve, hold, reject and comment from the site, with no install. If you want staff photographing receipts for expense claims, Lightyear covers that and PayKicker does not.

Does PayKicker work with MYOB like Lightyear does?

Yes. PayKicker connects to MYOB Business and AccountRight, and to Xero. Suppliers, accounts, jobs and tax codes come from your file, and approved bills go back as bills ready to pay. Lightyear connects to MYOB AccountRight and Acumatica.

Can I switch from Lightyear to PayKicker?

Yes. Connect Xero or MYOB, point your supplier emails at your PayKicker invoices address, and new bills arrive read and coded. Everything Lightyear already exported stays in your books, and PayKicker learns your coding from the suppliers already in your file.
