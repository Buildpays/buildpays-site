PayKicker vs Traild for supplier invoices

Both read supplier invoices, get them approved and post them to Xero or MYOB. Traild is a mid-market, fraud-first platform sold by demo, with a payments product and connectors to large ERPs. PayKicker publishes its price, reconciles the supplier statement that arrives by email, matches the invoice to the docket and the job, and runs on the same account as rostering and payroll for crews. Here is how the two compare for an Australian business on Xero or MYOB.

The supplier statement

At the end of the month the supplier sends a statement, and someone has to find out why it does not match. In Traild you upload the statement, its lines are matched to the invoices Traild holds, and a person reviews the misses and ticks the statement as reconciled. Payments and credits are not part of the match.

In PayKicker the statement arrives by email at your invoices address and reconciles itself: each line is found among the bills in PayKicker and the payments and credits in Xero or MYOB, a small difference is written off within a tolerance you set, and the lines that are truly missing become an email to the supplier asking for those invoices, drafted and waiting for you to send. The statement sits in its own tab with every line's state in plain words.

The invoice and the site

Traild matches an invoice to the purchase order and the goods receipt in the accounting system. For a builder or a subcontractor that is often not where the truth is. The truth is the delivery docket the foreman signed, the hours on the roster, the job the crew was on that day.

PayKicker holds those. An invoice is matched to the docket photo as well as the order, coded to the job the crew worked, and checked against what the site recorded. The approval goes to the person who runs that job, on their phone, and a delegated approval is one press, not two.

Which one fits

  • Pick Traild if you run NetSuite, SAP Business One, SYSPRO, IFS or MYOB Acumatica, want supplier payments made from inside the AP tool, and have a finance team that buys by demo and procurement.
  • Pick PayKicker if you are on Xero or MYOB Business and AccountRight, want to know the price before you call, want the supplier statement handled as well as the invoice, and run jobs where the docket and the roster decide whether a bill is right.

Questions

What does Traild cost?

Traild publishes no price. Its website, its Xero App Store listing and its MYOB app listing all say request a demo or request pricing (read 31 October 2026). PayKicker publishes its price: $9.99 a month inc GST for up to 20 supplier invoices, $29.99 up to 100, $69.99 up to 400 and $149 up to 1,000, with 14 days free and no card to start.

Does PayKicker reconcile supplier statements like Traild?

PayKicker goes further. Email the statement to your invoices address and it reconciles itself against the bills in PayKicker and the payments and credits in Xero or MYOB, within a tolerance you set, and drafts the email asking the supplier for the invoices you never received. Traild's statements tab takes an upload, matches lines to invoices only, and a person ticks the statement as reconciled.

Does PayKicker pay suppliers?

No. PayKicker posts the approved bill to Xero or MYOB and you pay from there or from your bank, as you do now. Traild sells a separate payments product, Traild Pay, that moves money through a payments partner for a fee.

Does PayKicker check for fraud?

Every bill is checked before anyone can approve it: the ABN, GST that adds up, a duplicate in PayKicker or already in Xero or MYOB, a bank account that differs from the one on file for that supplier, an invoice date out of range, and the purchase order it names. A bank-details difference says in plain words to confirm with the supplier by phone on a known number before paying.

Can I switch from Traild to PayKicker?

Yes. Connect Xero or MYOB, point your supplier emails at your PayKicker invoices address, and new bills arrive read and coded. Everything Traild already posted stays in your books. PayKicker learns your coding from the bills you approve and from the suppliers already in your file.
