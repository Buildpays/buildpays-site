Questions.

What counts as an invoice?

Every bill or credit note PayKicker reads for you and you submit. Reading the same file again is free.

Do the prices include GST?

Yes. $9.99 is what you pay, and you get a tax invoice each month.

What about invoices with GST and GST-free items?

PayKicker detects them automatically. When the GST on an invoice is neither 10% of the amount before GST nor nil (a supermarket order, fresh food with packaging, a mixed hardware run), it splits the bill into a taxable line and a GST-free line that add up to the invoice, each on the right tax code. If your chart keeps account pairs such as “with GST” and “GST-free”, each line goes to its own account. Nobody works out the split by hand.

What happens when the trial ends?

It ends after 14 days or 8 invoices read, whichever comes first. Add a card to keep reading; the trial's invoices stay free. New invoices are kept in PayKicker until you do.

How am I charged?

By card, once a month, for the band that month landed in. The card is saved and charged by Stripe; PayKicker never sees the number.

Which accounting software?

Xero, and MYOB Business or AccountRight. Your suppliers, accounts, jobs and tax codes come from there, and approved bills go back there.

Can I stop?

Any time, from Settings. You pay for the month so far, and the charges stop there. Everything already in Xero or MYOB stays there.
