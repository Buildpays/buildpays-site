1STEP 1 · BEFORE YOUR FIRST DAY

It starts with a text

No app store, no password. Tap the link, fill in your details once, snap photos of your tickets — the office checks them.

5STEP 5 · HOLIDAYS, RDOs & RAIN

Some days pay themselves

Public holiday or RDO you didn't work? Don't submit anything — it's paid automatically. Rained off? The office records it and your day is made up.

Kept working after the wet call-off? Those hours are double time — automatically.

7THE GOLDEN RULES

Four things to remember

1Submit every day you work, same day. Red day on your week strip = missing timesheet = chasing you on pay day.
2Real times, always. The agreement pays you right from honest times — overtime, allowances, the lot. Edited times get flagged for a second look, so just put what happened.
3PH, RDO, approved leave — submit nothing. Those days pay themselves. If you actually worked one, tell your allocation manager.
4Something wrong? Say so early. Wrong site, wrong hours, ticket expiring — your allocation manager or the office fixes it in minutes before pay day, not after.

Paid correctly, every week. That's the whole point.
