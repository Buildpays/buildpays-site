Square salesMYOB Opening soonLive

Each shop's day,
in MYOB every night.

Every Square location's day goes into MYOB as one sales invoice, and every payout is settled with Square's fees on their own accounts. Set it up for me finds or makes every account, customer and job it needs.

What it posts

  • One sales invoice per location per day, tax-inclusive, to the location's own customer, every line on the location's job.
  • Sales with GST and GST-free sales, net of discounts; discounts on their own line when you give them an account.
  • Card surcharges and service charges on their own accounts; cash rounding GST-free; tips (no GST) and gift cards sold kept apart, and a gift card redeemed taken off what you owe on gift cards.
  • Card payments into your Square clearing account, cash into your cash account.
  • A day with more refunded than sold goes in as one general journal.
  • Each payout: what Square kept (fees, third-party fees, Square Capital repayments, chargebacks, holds, adjustments), each on its own account and the fees on the location's job, as one spend money out of Square clearing; the payout itself as a transfer to your bank.
  • Every entry is found again before it is written, so a retry never doubles.

What your MYOB file needs

Square clearingA Bank account.

Supplier invoicesMYOB / Xero Live

Forward the bill.
It lands coded.

Email a bill to your own invoices address or drop in the PDF. Two readers check every figure, the right person approves, and the bill lands in Xero or MYOB ready to pay.

What it posts

  • Each approved bill as a bill in Xero or MYOB, ready to pay, against the supplier found by ABN and name.
  • Lines coded to account, job and tax code the way you coded that supplier before.
  • A bill with GST and GST-free items split onto the right tax codes, the two lines adding up to the invoice.
  • Credit notes too. Totals, GST and duplicates checked first, including bills already in Xero or MYOB.

Bank lines coded,
so the feed matches itself.
