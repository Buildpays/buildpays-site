PayKicker for Xero.
How the connection works.

One connection, made once by an owner or admin. PayKicker reads your suppliers, chart of accounts, tax rates and tracking categories from Xero, and posts approved supplier bills and daily sales back, coded. This page is the manual: connecting, what moves, permissions, disconnecting, help.

Connecting.

  1. Start from Xero or from PayKicker

    New to PayKicker: press Sign up with Xero. Xero asks you to sign in and to allow PayKicker; your name, email and business name come across, so there is nothing to retype. Already set up: open Company → Accounting setup and press Connect Xero.

  2. Pick the organisation

    If your Xero login has several organisations, choose the one this PayKicker business posts to. One organisation per business; a second business in PayKicker connects its own.

  3. PayKicker reads the file

    Suppliers, the chart of accounts, tax rates and the tracking category you use for jobs are read straight away and refreshed every night. The connection card shows the organisation's name and ● Connected.

  4. Work as usual

    Nothing changes in Xero until a bill is fully approved in PayKicker or a day's sales close. Then it lands in Xero, coded, with the document attached.

What moves, and which way.

  • Read from Xero

    Contacts marked as suppliers (name, ABN, bank details on file), the chart of accounts with each account's tax rate, tax rates, tracking categories and their options (your jobs), purchase orders when purchase-order matching is switched on, and bills already in Xero, so a duplicate is caught before it is posted again. With the Cashflow add-on: bank balances, open sales invoices and open bills, read only.

  • Written to Xero

    Supplier invoices: a fully approved bill becomes an awaiting payment bill (or a credit note) on the matched supplier, each line coded to the account, tax rate and tracking option you chose, the PDF attached. Sales sync: each shop's day from Square or Shopify becomes one sales invoice with its payments, posted to the clearing and cash accounts you mapped. Nothing is posted as paid that was not.

  • Never

    PayKicker never pays anyone, never changes a bill you keyed in Xero yourself, and never creates or edits suppliers. If a send fails, the bill waits in PayKicker with the reason, and you press Send again when it is fixed.

  • Permissions asked

    Who you are (name and email, at sign-up only), read contacts, read settings (accounts, tax rates, tracking), bills and credit notes, attachments, and the right to keep the connection alive between visits. The sales sync adds sales invoices, payments and bank transactions. PayKicker asks only for what the products you switched on need.

Disconnecting, from either side.

  • From PayKicker

    Company → Accounting setup → Disconnect. PayKicker removes itself from your organisation's Connected apps in Xero and deletes the tokens it held. Approved bills not yet sent stay in PayKicker, marked Not sent, until you connect again.

  • From Xero

    Settings → Connected apps → PayKicker → Disconnect. PayKicker notices within a day: the connection card changes to needs renewing and says it was disconnected from inside Xero. Nothing is sent until an owner or admin reconnects.

  • What stays

    Everything already posted stays in Xero as it is. Your PayKicker records stay until you close the account. Reconnecting the same organisation picks up where it left off.

Keeping it safe.

  • Your Xero password is never seen

    Connecting happens on Xero's own sign-in page. PayKicker receives a token, encrypted at rest and renewed automatically, never your password.

  • Only owners and admins

    Connecting and disconnecting are owner and admin actions. Every connection, disconnection and send is written to the company's audit trail with who did it and when.

  • Checked before it posts

    Every bill is checked for duplicates (in PayKicker and in Xero), a changed bank account, a wrong ABN, GST that does not add up and an invoice date out of range before anyone can approve it.

Questions.

Which Xero organisations can connect?

Any Xero organisation on a plan that includes bills (purchases). The person connecting needs a Xero role that can manage connected apps (an Adviser, or a Standard user allowed to).

Does PayKicker move money?

No. PayKicker never makes a payment. It posts approved bills, sales invoices and their payments as records; you pay suppliers from Xero or your bank as you do now.

What happens to my data if I disconnect?

Everything already posted stays in Xero. PayKicker deletes its Xero tokens straight away and removes itself from your Connected apps. Your PayKicker records stay until you close the account.

Can I connect one organisation to two PayKicker businesses?

Yes, each business connects on its own. PayKicker sends one bill to one organisation at a time, so two businesses posting to the same file never clash.

Does the Xero connection cost extra?

No. It is part of every PayKicker plan. Xero charges nothing for connecting an app.

I use MYOB, not Xero.

PayKicker connects to MYOB Business and AccountRight the same way. See the integrations page.
