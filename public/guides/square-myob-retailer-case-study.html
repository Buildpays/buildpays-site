Written 26 October 2026.

Before: daily takings by hand

The business is a Melbourne retailer with two shops, both on Square, with its books in MYOB. Each day's takings were entered into MYOB from Square's reports by hand.

With two shops, that is two entries for every day traded, and then each Square payout, less Square's fees, to match against the bank. The How to get Square sales into MYOB guide sets out that job step by step.

Now: every night, automatically

The business connected Square and its MYOB file to PayKicker, with each shop set up on its own job. Since mid-October 2026 PayKicker has posted each shop's day into MYOB automatically every night.

  • Around 200 orders a day across both shops, and close to 3,000 orders in its first two weeks.
  • Each shop on its own job in MYOB, so each shop's sales sit apart in the same file.
  • Every Square payout settled, with Square's fees on their own account and the payout as a transfer to the bank.
  • Each day checked against Square before it posts: the lines must add up to Square's own order totals and the GST must equal Square's, or the day is held with the reason instead of posted.

What posts each night

For each shop, one tax-inclusive sales invoice for the day, every piece of the day on its own account and every line on the shop's job; and for each Square payout, Square's fees on their own account and the payout to the bank. The Square fees, tips, surcharges and GST in MYOB and Xero guide shows those lines on an example day.

Figures rounded; the business asked not to be named.
