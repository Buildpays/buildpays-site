Referral partner terms

Effective 11 October 2026  ·  Version 2026-10-11

These terms are between PAYKICKER PTY LTD (ABN 91 700 949 944) ("PayKicker", "we") and you, the referral partner. You agree to them when you tick the box at sign-up; we record the version and the time.

1. Who can be a partner

Anyone. You don't need an ABN. You give us your name, email and the bank account your fees are paid to, and confirm your email from the link we send. The link opens your partner page without a password: keep it to yourself.

2. A referral

A business is referred by you when it signs up for PayKicker through your link, or types your code when it signs up. From then on it is tagged to you for good; it cannot be moved to another partner. A business already on PayKicker can be tagged only by us, and only if it has no partner. A sign-up using your own email is not tagged.

3. What the business gets

75% off its first 3 bills that carry a charge. A month with nothing to pay does not count.

4. What you earn

15% of the total (including GST) the referred business pays on each of its first 12 bills that carry a charge, for every PayKicker subscription it takes. A fee is earned when that bill is paid. Nothing is earned on a bill that is not paid or is voided. If a payment is refunded, we take the fee on it off your next payment and show why on your page.

5. How you are paid

Monthly, by direct deposit to the account on your page, from the 8th of the month, once at least $50 is owed; a smaller amount carries forward. When a business's 12th bill is paid we pay whatever is owed. Each payment is GST-inclusive: it is the whole amount we pay, nothing is added for GST, and no invoice is needed — we email a statement with each payment. Your own tax on what you receive is your responsibility. Keep your bank details up to date on your page; we email you whenever they change.

6. Telling the business

You must tell each business you refer that you receive a referral fee from PayKicker. If you are a registered tax or BAS agent, the Tax Practitioners Board's Code of Professional Conduct applies to you. PayKicker also tells the business, at sign-up, in its welcome email and on its billing page.

7. Fair dealing

Don't make false or misleading claims about PayKicker, and don't create sign-ups that are not genuine businesses choosing PayKicker. If we reasonably believe these terms have been broken, we may suspend you: while you are suspended no new business is tagged to you and nothing is paid to you. We tell you why by email.

8. Changes and leaving

We will give you at least 30 days' notice by email before changing these terms. You can leave at any time by emailing us; fees already earned on paid bills are still paid.

9. Your details

We use your details to run the programme and to pay you, as our privacy policy describes. Your page shows the businesses you referred and what each has earned you.
