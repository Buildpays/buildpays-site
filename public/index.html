The payroll pillar

The agreement is 186 pages. Your payroll shouldn't need all of them open.

A CFMEU Victoria construction EBA sets flat double-time overtime, weekend minimum payments, RDO accruals on a 36-hour week, per-project site allowances, daily fares and travel, casual loadings and inclement weather banks. Getting one of them wrong in a spreadsheet means back-pay, disputes, and audits. PayKicker encodes the rules once, so every timesheet comes out right.

FAQ

Questions subcontractors ask us.

Is there a free trial?

No self-serve trial. Book a demo with your enterprise agreement and a recent pay run — if PayKicker fits, we set you up with a free pilot period alongside your existing process until the MYOB export matches to the cent.

Does it replace MYOB?

No — it feeds it. MYOB stays your system of record and holds every pay rate. We handle everything before the pay run: rostering, EBA-classified timesheets, approvals, and an import file matched to your payroll categories and job codes.

Which enterprise agreements do you support?

The engine is built on the CFMEU Victoria construction & general pattern used across formwork, structure and civil agreements. During setup we configure it to your specific agreement.

How do workers use it?

A web app they add to their phone's home screen — no app store, no passwords. They see their roster, submit a timesheet in under 30 seconds, and get a text when tomorrow's site is confirmed.

Can we take just the dockets, without payroll?

Yes — the platform is modular. Dayworks dockets and cost reports on their own at a flat monthly fee, with rostering, onboarding and full payroll available as you need them.
