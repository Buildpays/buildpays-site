Questions.

Which accounting apps?

Xero, and MYOB Business and AccountRight. Nothing else yet.

No. Supplier invoices are sold on their own; start them yourself. Square sales sync will be too when it opens.

Does PayKicker touch my money?

No. It reads and writes accounting entries. Payments, payouts and bank transfers happen in your bank and in Square.
