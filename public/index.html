Questions.

Which accounting apps?

Xero and MYOB (MYOB Business and AccountRight). Nothing else yet.

Do I need the payroll part to use the rest?

No. Supplier invoices and Square sales are sold on their own, and you sign up for them yourself. The crew system is set up with you after a demo.

Does PayKicker touch my money?

No. It reads and writes accounting entries. Payments, payouts and bank transfers happen in your bank and in Square, never in PayKicker.

Where is my data?

In an Australian region, with encrypted backups. The Privacy Policy has the detail.

Is there a trial for the crew system?

Not a self-serve one. Book a demo; if PayKicker fits your company, the pilot period is on us.
