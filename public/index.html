Questions

Which accounting apps?

Xero, and MYOB Business or AccountRight. Nothing else yet.

Do I need the payroll part to use the rest?

No. Supplier invoices and Square sales sync are sold on their own, without the crew system. Start the invoices trial yourself.

Does PayKicker touch my money?

No. It reads and writes accounting entries. Payments, payouts and bank transfers happen in your bank and in Square.

Where is my data?

In the Australian region, with encrypted backups. The Privacy Policy has the detail.

Is there a trial for the crew system?

No self-serve trial. Book a demo with your enterprise agreement and a recent pay run; if PayKicker fits, the pilot period is on us, run alongside your current process until the pay run matches to the cent.
