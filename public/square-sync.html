Questions.

Does it work with MYOB AccountRight, MYOB Business and Xero?

Yes. PayKicker connects through MYOB's own sign-in, so it works with MYOB Business and with AccountRight files you open online. In Xero each shop's day goes in the same way, as a sales invoice on that shop's tracking option.

I have more than one Square location. Is that extra?

No. Every plan takes any number of shops, and the price follows your total orders across all of them. Each shop's day posts as its own invoice on its own job, with its own Square fees, so each shop has its own figures.

What if a day doesn't go through?

A day whose figures don't match Square's own totals is held with the reason and never posted. If MYOB or Xero doesn't answer, PayKicker tries that day again by itself on the nights that follow. Anything that didn't go through is emailed to your owners and admins after the night's run, and nothing is ever posted twice.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. It's billed monthly with no contract. Everything already posted stays in your MYOB or Xero file.
