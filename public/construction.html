Questions subcontractors ask us.

Is there a free trial?

For rostering, dockets and payroll, no self-serve trial. Book a demo with your enterprise agreement and a recent pay run. If PayKicker fits, we set you up with a free pilot period alongside your existing process until the pay run matches to the cent. Supplier invoices has a 14-day free trial you start yourself.

Does it replace Xero or MYOB?

No, it feeds them. Xero or MYOB stays your payroll and holds every pay rate. We handle everything before the pay run: rostering, EBA-classified timesheets and approvals. Then one click sends the pay run into your payroll, matched to your pay items and job codes, and turns signed dockets into an invoice.

Which enterprise agreements do you support?

The engine is built on the CFMEU Victoria construction & general pattern used across formwork, structure and civil agreements. During setup we configure it to your specific agreement.

How do workers use it?

A web app they add to their phone's home screen: no app store, no passwords. They see their roster, submit a timesheet in under 30 seconds, and get a text when tomorrow's site is confirmed.

Can we take just the dockets, without payroll?

Yes. There are three modules: rostering & inductions, dayworks dockets & cost reports, and EBA payroll, which runs on top of rostering. Take dockets alone, rostering alone, or rostering with payroll at a flat monthly fee, or the full system per active employee. Onboarding and ticket tracking are included with every plan.
